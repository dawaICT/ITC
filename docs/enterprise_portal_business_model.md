# Business model

The Skills and Enterprise Portal is a permanent institutional platform for discovery, verification, introductions, follow-up and outcome measurement.

**Institution-led connections (full spec):** [`enterprise_portal_institution_led_connections.md`](enterprise_portal_institution_led_connections.md) — partners, external opportunities, matching, consent-based referrals, engagement tracking, and separated outcome metrics.

## In scope (v1)
- Verified directory of skills, products, services, innovations, employment profiles and investment-ready proposals
- Portal-mediated expressions of interest and lead management
- Evidence-backed outcomes (jobs, orders, contracts, partnerships, funding received)
- Roadmap: institution-mediated employer and business connections (see institution-led connections doc)

## Out of scope (v1)
- Banking, escrow, investment brokerage, checkout/commissions, delivery logistics, guaranteed returns

## Monetization flags (disabled)
- `ENTERPRISE_MONETIZATION_ENABLED=false`
- `ENTERPRISE_PAID_FEATURED_LISTINGS=false`
- `ENTERPRISE_TRANSACTION_COMMISSION_ENABLED=false`

Participation is free for students and graduates in the initial version.
