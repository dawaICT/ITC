# Business rules

- Academic access ≠ enterprise access
- Membership must be active for participant tools
- Opportunity statuses change only through `ep_transition_opportunity`
- Technical verification ≠ publication
- Interests ≠ outcomes
- Investment readiness cannot be self-declared as a public investment-ready label
- Major published edits force `update_required`
- Type-specific validation is enforced server-side
- Public queries filter `published` only
